Before you place a bid

Understand your vehicle budget, additional costs, and the instruction you would give BEST CAR STAR when real bidding becomes available.

1. Currency and your maximum

All amounts entered on this website are in US dollars (USD). Your maximum is the most you authorize for the vehicle itself, not your total import budget.

Korean auction purchases are priced in KRW. Website conversions are estimates. Before a real bid, BEST CAR STAR must confirm the exchange rate, its validity period, and the corresponding KRW vehicle limit with you. A changed rate must not increase your authorized USD vehicle maximum without your agreement.

2. Fees and total cost

CostHow it is treated
Vehicle purchaseActual winning price, up to your approved maximum.
Auction-house feeAdditional; varies by auction and vehicle. Unconfirmed fees are not zero fees.
Inland transportAdditional; depends on the vehicle location and delivery destination.
BEST CAR STAR handlingAdditional; the current demo calculator uses USD 250. A real-service quote must be confirmed.
Export documents, loading, shipping, insurance and destination chargesConfirm each item in the written quote. They are not all included in the website estimate.

The displayed estimate is not a final invoice. It currently adds the maximum vehicle bid, estimated auction fee, inland transport and handling. Export shipping is excluded. For “Others”, the auction fee is unconfirmed and excluded from the calculation.

Example: a USD 10,000 maximum authorizes up to USD 10,000 for the vehicle; it does not cap fees or shipping at that amount. Additional charges must be disclosed and agreed separately before real bidding.

3. Deposits and payment

No money is required to test demo bids. The deposit policy for real auctions is not yet published.

Before accepting a real bid, BEST CAR STAR must confirm the deposit amount and currency, due date, verified payment details, whether it is credited toward the purchase, refund conditions and timing, and any deductions or forfeiture conditions. The balance-payment deadline must also be provided.

Do not send a deposit based only on a demo listing. No refund or forfeiture rule is implied by this guide.

4. What confirming a maximum bid means

Today: confirmation saves a practice bid only. It does not authorize a purchase or send a bid to an auction house.

Once real bidding is enabled and you accept the vehicle-specific terms: your confirmed maximum will instruct BEST CAR STAR to bid on the identified vehicle on your behalf, up to the approved vehicle-only limit, and complete the purchase if successful under those agreed terms. It will not authorize exceeding your maximum, substituting another vehicle, or adding undisclosed charges.

A maximum bid does not guarantee a purchase. If the vehicle is won, the purchase and payment obligations will follow the terms you accepted before submission. A separate final approval after winning must not be assumed.

5. Changes, withdrawal and deadlines

For the current demos, you may edit or withdraw in My Bids before the displayed deadline. Changes require a confirmation step. Withdrawn bids remain in your history but are excluded from active bids.

At the deadline, editing and withdrawal close. For real auctions, the website cutoff must be confirmed in advance and may be earlier than the auction-house deadline. After BEST CAR STAR submits an external bid, withdrawal may no longer be possible; that rule must be disclosed before accepting your instruction.

The blue countdown uses the saved listing deadline. Current deadlines are demonstrations, not a live auction-house feed.
