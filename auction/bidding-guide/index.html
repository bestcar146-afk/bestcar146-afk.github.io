1. Currency and your maximum

All bids and estimated costs are shown in USD. Your maximum bid covers the vehicle only. Auction fees, inland transport and handling are shown separately on the vehicle page.

Korean auction prices are converted to USD using the exchange rate shown after the auction-fee tables.

2. Fees and total cost

Your estimated subtotal includes the vehicle bid, auction-house fee, inland transport and BEST CAR STAR handling service. Export shipping, insurance, loading and destination charges are confirmed separately with the admin. The estimate is not a final invoice.

3. Deposits and payment

This website does not collect or process payments. Use “Request access · WhatsApp” in your account to contact the admin for the deposit amount, payment instructions and refund conditions.

The admin confirms payment externally and activates your bidding privileges. Until activation, your bidding button remains grey. Creating an account or sending a message does not activate access automatically.

Confirm the deposit currency, due date, credit toward the purchase, refund timing, deductions and balance-payment deadline with the admin before paying. No payment is required for demo listings.

4. What submitting a maximum bid authorizes

For a real auction under the agreed terms, your confirmed maximum authorizes BEST CAR STAR to bid on the identified vehicle on your behalf up to your vehicle-only limit, and complete the purchase if successful.

It does not authorize exceeding your maximum, substituting another vehicle or adding undisclosed charges. A bid does not guarantee a purchase. If successful, you must pay according to the terms agreed before submission; a separate approval after winning is not assumed.

Demo bids are practice only and do not authorize a purchase.

5. Changes, withdrawal and deadlines

Review your maximum before confirming. You may edit or withdraw your bid in My Bids before the displayed bidding deadline. Each change requires confirmation.

At the deadline, editing and withdrawal close. The website deadline may be earlier than the auction-house deadline so BEST CAR STAR can process your instruction.

Once a bid has been submitted to the auction house, changes or cancellation depend on that auction’s rules and may no longer be possible. Contact the admin on WhatsApp immediately; a message alone does not confirm cancellation.

Withdrawn bids remain in your history and are excluded from active bidding. Deactivating bidding privileges blocks new bids and edits; it does not automatically cancel an existing bid.
